Music

For as long as I can remember, music has been a huge part of my life. Stemming from my dad’s talent, listening, learning, and making music has been a hobby of mine for over 8 years, starting with GarageBand beats and stock sounds. In 2022, I bought a guitar and have been learning and playing it since. As of 2025, I’ve started experimenting with more genres, fusing hip hop and house.
